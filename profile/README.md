### Who we are
Peebify is a small team of players who got tired of using a separate launcher for every game. So we built one that does it all, and we keep building on it with the people who use it.
### What we do
- **Peebify Launcher.** One place to install, update and repair your games, with mods, wallpapers, news and playtime tracking built in.
- **The Peebify website and services.** These run [peebify.net](https://peebify.net/) and the wallpaper and news feeds the launcher shows.
- **Open source.** The launcher's core is public as [Peebify Launcher Lite](https://github.com/Peebify-Team/peebify-launcher-lite) under GPL-3.0, so anyone can use it.
